Digital Citizenship

What is Digital Citizenship?

Digital citizenship means using the internet, technology, and digital devices in a safe, responsible, respectful, and ethical way.

What is Digital Law?

Digital law refers to the rules and laws that govern how people use technology, the internet, and digital information.

What is AUP?

It is a set of rules that explain how people are allowed to use a school, workplace, or organization's technology and internet.

What is Plagiarism?

Plagiarism is using someone else's words, ideas, or work without giving them proper credit.

Simple definition: Plagiarism is taking someone else's work or ideas and presenting them as your own.

What is Academic Dishonesty?

Academic dishonesty is cheating or using dishonest methods to gain an unfair advantage in school or college.

Simple definition: Academic dishonesty is being dishonest in your schoolwork to get a better grade or an unfair advantage.

What are the rules for AI Generated Content?

Rules for AI-generated content are guidelines about how students can use AI tools (such as ChatGPT) for schoolwork.
